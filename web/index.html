NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

Privacy

  • NetScope Web runs in your browser; the static app has no backend and does not store your information.
  • The public-IP request goes to ipify. The service receives the request and can see its source IP; NetScope does not make claims about the service's logging.
  • DNS Lookup sends the domain you enter to Google Public DNS over HTTPS. Its handling is governed by Google's service policies.
  • Browser restrictions prevent access to your MAC address, gateway, configured DNS servers, DHCP settings, and full adapter details.
  • No geolocation permission, precise location, analytics, or LAN scan is used.
